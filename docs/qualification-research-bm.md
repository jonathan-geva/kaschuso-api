# Public BM1 TALS research — GIBS Solothurn / Olten

Research date: 10 November 2026. These rule-research notes use public sources only and reproduce no private worksheet or student values. A separate authorised private verification informed portal integration; its captures and records are not part of this research document or repository.

## Scope and decisive findings

- For **GIBS Solothurn BM1 Technik / Informationstechnologie**, the public school timetable is an **8-semester model**. Olten's current public blank calculator is a **6-semester model**. They share nine final subjects, but their semester ranges differ.
- The relevant old law is BMV of 24 June 2009 (2016 consolidated version), including MAP 2019-era cohorts. The revised BMV of 13 June 2025 takes effect 1 March 2026; **do not apply its new rounding rules to existing cohorts**.
- Solothurn's current cantonal regulation §28 explicitly retains old law for BM courses begun **before school year 2026/27**. Old-law BM exams last take place in **2033**. The federal transition is based on studies begun before 1 March 2026; the cantonal school-year rule resolves the normal Solothurn autumn-intake case.
- An exact publicly hosted blank original entitled “BM1 TALS Qualifikationsverfahren ab MAP2019” was **not located**. A newer official blank calculator, an archived historical official curriculum, a live 2023 Solothurn timetable, a live MAP2026 exam guide, and both federal legal texts were located. Do not represent the newer Olten sheet as the exact original or use its six-semester ranges for Solothurn.

## Old-law final qualification formulas (MAP2019-era / existing cohorts)

Notation: `Rhalf(x)` = nearest whole or half mark, `R1(x)` = one decimal place. Read official semester marks as fixed whole/half values. Ties convention is an implementation assumption unless the actual school calculator confirms it; conventional half-up would be `floor(2*x + 0.5)/2` and `floor(10*x + 0.5)/10` for positive marks. Use exact decimal/rational arithmetic to avoid floating-point boundary changes.

**Old BMV16 + BMV24 and MAP2026 guide2.1:**

- `ERFA_subject = Rhalf(mean(all official semester marks in that subject))`.
- `Exam_subject = Rhalf(single exam performance or mean of the exam performances)`; where written and oral both exist, use the mean before this rounding.
- `Final_subject = Rhalf((ERFA_subject + Exam_subject)/2)` for the six exam subjects.
- For the two complementary subjects, `Final_subject = ERFA_subject` (no regular final exam).
- `Final_IDA = Rhalf((ERFA_IDAF + IDPA)/2)`.
- `Overall_BM = R1(mean(the nine Final_subject values))`.

Each of the following **nine final subjects has weight 1/9**. Groundwork group4/9; specialisation group2/9; complementary group2/9; interdisciplinary1/9. Do not weight final subjects by weekly lessons, by how many semester marks they have, or by how many raw tests were taken.

### Naturwissenschaften

- Physics and Chemistry are **parts of one final subject**, not two of the nine. They count as one subject for insufficient-mark counts and the deficit sum.
- Source5 exam table (pp7–8): Physics80min, Chemistry40min; respective part marks to tenths. `Exam_NW = Rhalf((2*Physics_exam_tenth + Chemistry_exam_tenth)/3)`.
- Source4 timetable: Physics in semesters3–8, Chemistry in5–8;160/80 total lessons. This confirms2:1 lesson totals, **but does not alone prove how raw semester performance means must be combined**.
- Source6 explicitly defines Olten NW semester3/4 as Physics and semester5/6 as the equal mean of Physics/Chemistry, then averages NW semester3–6. This is an Olten-specific public formula, not a proven Solothurn formula.
- Safe, verified Solothurn formula when official combined NW semester marks are supplied: `ERFA_NW = Rhalf(mean(NW_sem3, NW_sem4, NW_sem5, NW_sem6, NW_sem7, NW_sem8))`.
- **Open detail:** exact Solothurn “ab MAP2019” transformation from separate Physics/Chemistry semester inputs to the official combined NW semester note was not publicly verified. Do not invent a weighted overall ERFA by averaging all raw Physics/Chemistry marks or assume the exam's2:1 weighting applies separately to every semester. Use official NW marks or the authenticated blank original to settle this.

### IDAF and IDPA

- These belong to BM interdisciplinary work. They are **not EFZ überbetriebliche Kurse (üK)**. No üK marks enter the nine-subject BM average.
- Old federal BMV24(3),(5),(6): IDAF ERFA is the mean of its semester marks; IDPA is assessed through process/product/presentation; the final IDA mark is half IDAF ERFA and half IDPA.
- RLP2012§9.1.4.3: at least **two IDAF semester marks**, each based on at least **two assessed performances within that semester**, for BM1. Two-semester BM2 has a different provision (at least3 performances).
- Historical GIBS Solothurn school-specific concept (source7 p152): two works (more possible), two performance marks per work -> **one semester mark per work** -> the mean of the **two semester marks** gives IDAF ERFA. Work1 in semester3or4; work2 in5or6. Its IDAF semester marks do not affect promotion.
- Thus4 raw IDAF entries may represent2 pairs of assessments, not4 semester marks. The route `Rhalf(mean(Rhalf(mean(pair1)), Rhalf(mean(pair2))))` can differ from a direct one-stage mean of4 raw marks. Preserve the stages if raw marks are used. If official IDAF semester grades exist, use those directly.
- Source4 allocates IDPA lessons to7–8. Historical source7 describes beginning in7 and hand-in before the sport break; scheduling may change. The final counted contribution is **one IDPA note**, not a mean of2 invented IDPA semester notes.

### Final pass conditions

Old BMV25 refers to17(4); source6 prints the same conditions. All must hold:

1. `Overall_BM >= 4.0`.
2. `count(Final_subject <4.0) <=2`.
3. `sum(max(0,4.0-Final_subject)) <=2.0` across nine final subjects.

These checks use final subject marks; **IDA counts here**. No separate must-pass rule for every exam, IDPA alone, Math alone, Chemistry alone or Physics alone was found in the BM final criteria. EFZ completion is also required for issuance of the BM qualification (old BMV2/28, cantonal§23). Passing BM calculation alone does not assert EFZ completion.

### Semester promotion is a separate calculation

Old BMV17 / new BMV16: use only **subjects actually taught that semester**, with their official semester marks; exclude IDAF/IDA for promotion. Apply the same three numeric thresholds (overall to1decimal at least4, at most2insufficient subjects, deficit sum≤2), but over that semester's taught subjects. Do not reuse the fixed9-subject final average. IDPA/IDA is not a ninth semester-promotion subject. Physics/Chemistry are combined into the one NW subject according to the school semester scheme.

BM1 old-law permits one provisional promotion; a second failure excludes the learner. This status requires prior promotion history, not merely the current marks. A projected mean of ongoing individual exams is an estimate until the official semester grade is fixed. Missing/pending data is not0; valid marks are1–6.

## Changes for starts from school year2026/27

New BMV23 materially changes rounding:

- Semester grade: whole/half, based on at least2separately graded performances.
- Subject ERFA: mean of all official semester grades, rounded to **one decimal**.
- One exam performance: exam note whole/half.
- Several exam performances: their mean rounded to **one decimal**.
- Subject final note: whole/half from50%exam+50%ERFA; subjects without exam equal ERFA subject final rounding towhole/half.
- IDPA: whole/half. BM1 IDAF ERFA: mean ofIDAF semester grades, rounded to **one decimal**. IDA final: whole/half from50%IDPA+50%IDAF ERFA.
- BM overall: one decimal; same three final pass criteria.
- Certificate acceptance moves from an SBFI recognition decision to canton-defined accepted exams (newBMV22). An accepted certificate may replace the final exam, and attendance can be partly/fully waived, but **ERFA cannot be waived on that basis**.

Do not silently migrate an existing cohort's stored results to this version. Version by BM start date/cohort; if start is unknown, request/retain a provisional profile rather than assert applicability.

## Language certificate variants and limits

Source5 is specificallyTALS: French oral internal exam OR recognised international **B1** exam; English written+oral OR recognised international **B2** exam. Source6 names **DELF B1** and **FCE/Cambridge First** and refers to cantonal implementation of Recommendation11 dated14September2024. It does not provide that conversion table. It does not establish that this recommendation's exact date/text remains valid for every historic certificate.

OldBMV23: accepted diploma exam replaces the **entire final language exam**, not ERFA. A diploma taken before BM studies only replaces the exam if it actually led to an awarded diploma recognised when taken. Results from a qualifying diploma offered during the course are converted to an exam note. Accepted type, exam date/cohort, obtained result and applicable conversion rules are distinct inputs. Never treat “has certificate” as automatic6 or omit ERFA.

The linked “Merkblatt Fremdsprachen BM1” on Olten's TALS page is actually titled **BM1 Typ Wirtschaft**, datedDecember2025, and requires FrenchDELF B2; **do not use that accidentally linked business-track sheet forTALS**. [Misleading linked file](https://bbzolten.so.ch/fileadmin/bbz-olten/KBS/BM_KBS/Merkblatt_Fremdsprachen_BM_1.pdf).

For a product under the currently established old cohort, the dependable minimal option is an internal exam mark or an already school-confirmed converted certificate exam mark, with source/date fields. Raw diploma-score conversion needs the exact canton/cohort table; public research here does not establish it. The [SKKBS calculator](https://www.skkbs-csepc.ch/diplomrechner) is a reference tool, and its notice reserves final confirmation to the canton/exam commission.
